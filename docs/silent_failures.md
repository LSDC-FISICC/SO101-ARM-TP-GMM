# Silent failures — catalogue

Failures that do not crash but corrupt results. Each entry: symptom, how it is detected
(test/assert), and status. Updated phase by phase.

| # | Failure | Why it is silent | Guard | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Joint units misread** (degrees vs `RANGE_M100_100`) | Both are floats of similar magnitude (±100); FK still returns plausible-looking poses | `infer_joint_units` (quantization 360/4095° + range evidence) runs at load; contradictory config raises. Tests: `tests/test_units.py` (both modes, float32, contradictory data), real-data test asserts DEGREES; grasp heights tight within ±1.5 cm (report §4) | guarded (Phase 0) |
| 2 | **Video vs state timestamp misalignment** (v3 packs many episodes per MP4) | Off-by-episode or off-by-frames returns a valid image of the wrong instant | `VideoReader` raises `FrameTimestampError` beyond 0.4 frame; episode offset `from_timestamp` applied in one place. Tests: synthetic brightness-coded video (`test_video_and_dataset.py`), real-data alignment; report §5: 0.00 ms on 8 episodes, span == length for all 400 | guarded (Phase 0) |
| 3 | **Gripper threshold without hysteresis** (phantom cycles) | Plateaus at 20 (holding) and 25–28 (small openings) hover near any single threshold | Hysteresis band 23.5/25.5 + debounce + plateau-level classification + min hold duration. Tests: `test_gripper.py` (noise at threshold, short holds, missed grasps) | guarded (Phase 0) |
| 3b | **Hold-level plateau in the air** (operator half-closes the leader on the way home) | Looks exactly like a grasp by gripper value alone (16 episodes, report §3) | Table-height gate on the contact (`grasp_max_z`), release-height gate (`release_min_z`); rejected candidates are listed with their reason. Tests: `test_contact_in_air_is_rejected`, real-data test (episodes 370, 374) | guarded (Phase 2) |
| 3c | **Release with a tiny opening** (< open threshold) | Cycle is lost (5 episodes) instead of mis-segmented | Hold → closed-empty above the table = quiet release at the end of the hold plateau. Tests: `test_quiet_release_above_table_is_recovered`, real data recovers exactly episodes 106, 143, 168, 221, 234 | guarded (Phase 2) |
| 4 | **Red hue wrap-around** | OpenCV hue wraps at 180: a single interval [0, 10] misses red pixels at 170–179, so detections shrink or vanish depending on the lighting | Red uses two intervals (or one wrapped interval `lo > hi`, supported by `hue_mask`). Test: `test_red_hue_wraps` | guarded (Phase 3) |
| 5 | **Arm or hand occluding the cubes in the reference frame** | A partially hidden cube still yields a blob, with a biased centre; a hand in the frame hides the cube or adds skin blobs | Scene observed just before motion onset, not at frame 0 (fixes 168/170, cube still in the hand); flags `arm_not_home` (TCP vs home), `scene_not_static` (pixel change in 0.5 s), `near_arm`, `partial_blob` (area < 0.7 × expected footprint). Flagged cycles are excluded from training. Tests: `test_scene_validation_flags`, `test_reference_frame_fixes_hand_in_first_frame` | guarded (Phase 3) |
| 6 | **90° yaw ambiguity of the cube / image mirroring** | `minAreaRect` angles jump by 90°; the image is mirrored w.r.t. the table, so using the image angle directly flips the sign | Yaw folded to [−45°, 45°); orientation pushed through the homography (`PixelToBase.yaw_to_base`). Checked against the gripper yaw at contact: median residual 7° via the homography vs 19° using the image angle. Tests: `test_fold_yaw_90`, `test_yaw_ambiguity_of_90_degrees`, `test_yaw_to_base_handles_mirroring` | guarded (Phase 3) |
| 7 | **Homography to the wrong plane** (table vs cube) / TCP ≠ cube centre | A homography fits either plane with small error; the bias (~h/H × distance from the nadir) appears only at execution | Homography fitted on the detected cube centroids ↔ FK cube centres (TCP + offset), so the plane is consistent by construction; a table-plane mapping applied to cubes is 16.6 mm off (median) vs FK. Tests: `test_homography_on_the_wrong_plane_is_biased`, `test_ransac_calibration_rejects_pushed_cubes` | guarded (Phase 3) |
| 7b | **Cube pushed by the gripper before the grasp** | The initial cube pose (the frame TP-GMM is conditioned on) differs from where the grasp happened; the demonstration looks valid | Vision vs FK cube centre > 20 mm → `cube_pushed`, excluded from training (25 cycles; frames in `vision_report.md`) | guarded (Phase 3) |
| 8 | **Positions outside the reachable workspace / joint limits** | An IK that clips to limits or returns its best effort produces a plausible but wrong joint trajectory | Joint limits = URDF ∪ observed range (the rest pose violates the URDF). `SO101IK` reports success only if position ≤ 1 mm and yaw ≤ 1° inside limits with the yaw defined; unreachable targets come back flagged with their real error (`test_unreachable_target_is_flagged_not_clipped`). Reachability map on the table in `kinematics_report.md`; inference must check feasibility per cube (Phase 5) | guarded (Phase 1) |
| 9 | **Camera pose over-trusted** | Lens position/tilt look precise but are poorly determined from a near-overhead planar fit | Bootstrap CIs (dataset_report §6); metric grid calibration with principal-point/height sensitivity table (camera_calibration.md) | documented |
| 11 | **Free redundant DOF in IK** (5 joints, position + yaw) | The TCP path is exact but the posture drifts (up to ~70° per joint vs the demonstration) or reaches configurations where the yaw is undefined | Soft approach-pitch target + task-priority polish; yaw-observability check in the success test; tests recover recorded joints when given the demonstrated pitch | guarded (Phase 1) |
| 10 | **FK metric scale vs reality** | A uniform scale error keeps trajectories smooth and plausible | Grid ↔ FK similarity fit reports scale 0.953; to be verified on the robot | open |
| 12 | **Yaw crossing ±180° inside the model** | The raw tool yaw sits near ±180° (the TCP x axis points back at the robot); 170/366 trajectories cross the cut, which would split a single behaviour into two far-apart clusters | Learned variable is the outward heading h = wrap(ψ + π), unwrapped per trajectory; angular means aligned modulo 2π before the product of Gaussians. Test: `test_angles_aligned_modulo_2pi_in_product` | guarded (Phase 4) |
| 13 | **90° cube symmetry mixing demonstrations** | The same grasp appears with frame yaws 90° apart, scrambling the approach direction in the cube frame | Training uses, per demo, the equivalent cube yaw closest to the grasp heading; execution picks it from the cube azimuth + learned offset (accuracy measured in Phase 5). Test: `test_heading_and_cube_symmetry` | guarded (Phase 4) |
| 14 | **Follower configured in the wrong units at execution** | A plan in degrees sent to a follower in `RANGE_M100_100` drives the arm to a different, plausible posture | `SO101Runner` connects with `use_degrees=True` and refuses a follower that is not in degree mode. Test: `test_runner_refuses_non_degree_follower` | guarded (Phase 5) |
| 15 | **Unreachable cube at inference** | Clipping or best-effort IK would execute a trajectory that misses the cube | `plan_from_image` skips a cube with any IK failure and reports where (`require_full_ik`); partial blobs are skipped too | guarded (Phase 5) |
| 16 | **State-dependent gripper closing in the air (LPV-DS)** | A smooth g(distance) map is almost closed a few cm from the target: after a push the jaws close in the air although the arm is still converging; offline tests (no perturbations) never show it | Gripper held open within 2 cm of the attractor and latched open once opened; it closes only on arrival. Found by the perturbation benchmark (20 % premature closures before the fix) | guarded |
| 17 | **Time-indexed gripper under delays** | A time-indexed policy closes the gripper when its clock says so, wherever the arm is: after a 2 s stall it closes 35–46 mm from the cube | Reported in `perturbation_report.md`; the LPV-DS (state-dependent, closes on arrival) is immune. Mitigation for TP-GMM: phase/time scaling from progress, or gating the closing on the distance to the cube | documented |
| 18 | **Convex-hull collision of the jaws (MuJoCo)** | MuJoCo collides meshes by their convex hulls: the hull of the part carrying the fixed jaw and that of the moving jaw fill the gap between the fingers (they overlap at every opening). The scene compiles and runs, the arm simply pushes cubes instead of grasping them | Each finger replaced by boxes fitted to 1 cm slices of its mesh (`sim/scene.py: finger_boxes`). Test: `test_finger_gap_follows_gripper_command` (tips touch at 0, 4 cm apart near 26) | guarded (simulation) |
| 19 | **Vision cube position vs finger geometry** | The camera-based cube centre the policies were trained with is ~2 cm off the point between the fingers of the FK model (top face in perspective + joint calibration), consistently, so the offline metrics never see it; a simulator (or a re-calibrated camera) given the physical cube centre makes every grasp hit the cube with a finger | In simulation the known cube pose is expressed in the policy's convention (`sim/episode.py: policy_input`). On the robot: keep the camera calibration used in training, or re-calibrate it against grasps, not against a ruler | documented |
| 20 | **Table plane in FK coordinates** | In FK coordinates the (flat) table looks tilted by ~3.4° and 2.5 cm below the tape-grid estimate: a learned grasp height that is right on average is ~1 cm high far from the robot and ~1 cm low near it | Simulation table = plane fitted to the finger tips at the demonstrated grasps (`sim.table_plane`). On the robot: re-calibrate the joint offsets, or give the cube frame the z of this plane at the cube's (x, y) | open |
| 21 | **Shared temporary files in parallel simulation** | Workers writing the generated scene to the same file load each other's cube layouts: results look plausible but do not match their seeds | Unique temporary file per compilation (`build_model`); batch vs in-process results checked to match | guarded (simulation) |
| 22 | **Single release point, several cubes** | Every demonstration had one cube, so the policies release every cube at the same spot; the cubes stack and the third one falls out. Invisible in the offline evaluation (one cube per cycle) | Measured in `mujoco_sim.md`; mitigation: shift the box frame per cube (box slots, a task parameter changed at execution) | documented |
| 23 | **Camera view different from the calibration view** | Detection still works and every cube is planned "OK", but the pixel → base homography belongs to the dataset3 view: a photo with another zoom, another capture mode resized to 640×480, or a moved camera places every grasp off by centimetres, growing away from where the views agree. On the robot (2026-11): view scaled ×0.89, grasps off by 12–26 mm, two of three cubes hit with a fingertip | Each photo is aligned to the calibration view on the taped grid (`vision/register.py: correct_view`; `infer.py --register on` by default for TP-GMM, LPV-DS and IK planner; `CameraCubeSource` for SAC scratch and residual) and the homography is corrected; `scripts/capture_top.py` captures at 640×480 and reports the shift. Test: `test_moved_camera_is_corrected` | guarded |
| 24 | **Missed grasp executed open loop** | The plan is played to the end: the arm carries an empty gripper to the box, opens it and goes on to the next cube; the run looks complete. On the robot (2026-11) two of three cubes were missed this way | Every grasp is verified (measured vs commanded gripper ``settle_s`` after closing, optional wrist camera) and a missed cube is re-detected and planned again (`grasp.py`, `robot/pick_place.py`, `scripts/run_pick_place.py`; in MuJoCo `run_episode(replan=...)`). Tests: `test_grasp_check.py` | guarded |
